Write a brief the advocate can read in 60 seconds before this matter is heard on $hearing_date. The sources are the matter's record: the last order's summary, notes and voice notes, pending applications and tasks, client notes and documents.

- lines: at most 8 short lines, each in one section:
  - last_time: what happened at the last hearing and what the last order said;
  - pending: pending applications and open tasks;
  - client: what the client notes say that matters for this hearing;
  - carry: documents to carry.
- Every line cites its sources. Leave a section out when the sources say nothing for it.

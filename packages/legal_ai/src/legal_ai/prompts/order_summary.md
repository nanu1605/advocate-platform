Summarise the court order in the sources for an advocate in the case. Each source is one page of the order.

- title: the case title as the order gives it, for example "Ramesh Kumar v. State of MP". court, order_date and judges as the order states them; header_sources: the pages they are on.
- outcome: what the court did at this hearing, for example adjourned, notice issued, allowed, dismissed or disposed.
- gist: the order in two or three plain sentences.
- directions: every direction the court gave: what must be done, who must do it, and by when as the order words it ("within 4 weeks", "by 12.11.2026"). Only directions the order gives.
- next_date: the next date of hearing, if the order fixes one. It is not a direction: leave "List on …" out of directions.
- relief: relief granted or refused, if any.
- key_reasons: the court's main reasons, if the order gives them.
- authorities_cited_in_document: the judgments and statutory provisions the order itself cites, exactly as the order writes them. Never add one.
- next_steps_for_us: practical next steps for the advocate that follow from the directions and the next date.

Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. A date the order gives without its year is the first such date after the order's date.
